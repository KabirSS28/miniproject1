About Arnav

Campaign manager focused on digital reach and content performance

Arnav Bajoria is a Babson College graduate with experience building campaign reach for brands, influencers, and artists across the digital marketing space. With a strong background in content strategy and campaign execution, he helps clients grow their visibility through clear targeting, strong creative direction, and platform-aware social media planning.

Much of his campaign work comes through InClips Media, where he supports projects that require strategic planning, creator coordination, and audience growth. His work has had a deeper focus on sports, gambling, and music, while still supporting clients across multiple industries.
